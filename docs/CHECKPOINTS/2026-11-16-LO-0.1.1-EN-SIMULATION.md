## Evidence and outcome

Ran an isolated Lua 5.4 test harness against the exact 0.1.1 Lua source included in the ZIP (Git blob `75bbcdf9739d836163b47cf363d29e0c6f596c64`). The ZIP SHA-256 and every included Git blob checksum were checked against the feature branch.

**32 cases in total: 28 passed, 0 unexpected failures, 4 deliberately exploratory gaps.** Coverage:

- German `deDE`, English `enUS` and English fallback `enGB`/`esES`/missing `GetLocale`.
- Own loot (native German owner line and positive loot flags), exact violet color and locale-specific heading.
- Foreign owner with known German `Plündern: NAME`, and simulated English `Loot rights: NAME`, `Loot: NAME` and `Lootable by: NAME` where the group-roster name matches.
- Case-insensitive recognition of simulated English owner labels; exact red color.
- Empty state with no owner, unknown and blocked gray states, allowable interaction messages.
- MobInfo2 separate tooltip, matching GUID, rejection of stale GUID.
- Correct suppression on `UNIT_LOOT(same GUID,false)` and no suppression for an unrelated GUID.

**Exploratory inputs not recognized (four):**

1. Hypothetical `Loot: Mojo Burner` when there is no roster entry for Mojo Burner.
2. Hypothetical `Loot: Mojo Burner` when the roster knows only `Mojo`.
3. Hypothetical `Looting: Mojo Burner` even with matching roster.
4. Hypothetical native tooltip with a two-column `Loot` / `Mojo Burner` pair without roster.

These are demonstrated limits of this parser **for the supplied simulated inputs**, not evidence that the real English Forever client uses any of those four formats. Do not claim a verified English in-game bug or full compatibility based on a simulation alone.
