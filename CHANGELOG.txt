LootOwner 0.0.12 BETA CANDIDATE - WoW Forever 1.60.1
=====================================================
- First beta candidate, based on the 0.0.12 test build.
- Existing loot-owner recognition and tooltip behavior kept unchanged.
- Standalone operation; optional MobInfo2 compatibility.
- Own loot displayed in bright violet; confirmed foreign owner in red.
- Diagnostic-only chat commands from previous versions are absent.
- Known issue: after a group member completely loots a corpse, the
  foreign owner's label may remain due to missing authoritative state.
- No attempt to guess loot state or assign chat messages to a corpse.
- 0.0.12 gameplay check for own loot passed; group-empty edge remains open.
- This candidate changes documentation only; Lua and TOC are identical
  to the tested 0.0.12 TEST package.

Earlier internal diagnostic versions: 0.0.10 / 0.0.11.
Earlier test baseline: 0.0.9.
